Why Brussels, why now

Healthy longevity is reaching the European agenda

Heales has organised EHA from Brussels since 2012, a few streets from the institutions that set Europe's research and health priorities. This autumn, longevity research has come up in the European Parliament and in a dedicated EU research call.
